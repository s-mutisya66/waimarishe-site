The Process

From application to disbursement

Four steps, start to finish — most decisions made within two working days.

01

Apply

Fill the form on this site or visit our Kitengela office with your ID and business details.

02

Assessment

A loan officer reviews your application and, where needed, visits your business or group.

03

Approval

Approved applications are stamped and terms confirmed with you directly — no hidden charges.
